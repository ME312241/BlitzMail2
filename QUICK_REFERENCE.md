# BlitzMail 3D - Quick Reference

## Controls
- **W/A/S/D** - Move
- **Mouse** - Look around
- **Space** - Jump  
- **C** - Crouch
- **V** - Toggle camera view
- **ESC** - Exit

## How to Play
1. Pick up packages (brown boxes) by walking near them
2. Deliver packages to houses within 30 seconds
3. Earn points for faster deliveries (100 + time bonus)
4. Don't run out of time or lose a life!
5. Deliver all 5 packages to win

## Game Info
- **Starting Lives**: 3
- **Total Packages**: 5
- **Time per Package**: 30 seconds
- **Max Points per Delivery**: 400

## Visual Cues
- **Red Timer**: Less than 10 seconds remaining
- **Red Street Lights**: Warning - time running out!
- **Green/Yellow Timer**: You have time
- **Carried Package**: Visible in front of camera

## Tips
- Plan your route to houses before picking up packages
- Use V to toggle third-person view for better navigation
- Watch street lights - they turn red when time is low
- Jump to see over obstacles and scout locations
- Faster deliveries = higher scores!
